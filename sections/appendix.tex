\section{Derivation of the Residual Variance}
\label{app:variance}
Let a ray with unit direction $\ray$ meet a locally planar surface with unit normal
$\nrm$ at perpendicular distance $h$ from the camera, with incidence angle $\theta$
($\cos\theta=|\nrm\cdot\ray|$). The range to the plane is $r(\theta)=h/\cos\theta$,
so $\mathrm{d}r/\mathrm{d}\theta=h\sin\theta/\cos^{2}\theta=r\tan\theta$ exactly. A
pointing error $\delta\varphi$ in the plane of incidence therefore changes the
intersection range by $\delta r\approx r\tan\theta\,\delta\varphi$, whereas the
out-of-plane component has no first-order effect. With $\delta\varphi$ independent of
the range noise,
$\mathrm{Var}(r_{\mathrm{meas}}-r_{\mathrm{plane}})=\sigma_r^{2}+r^{2}\sigma_\varphi^{2}\tan^{2}\theta$,
which is \eqref{eq:vres}. The derivation assumes a locally planar surface, small errors
($\sigma_\varphi\tan\theta\ll1$) and independent Gaussian $\sigma_r$ and $\sigma_\varphi$.
The regularisation \eqref{eq:vreg} replaces $\tan^{2}\theta$ by
$\sin^{2}\theta/(\cos^{2}\theta+\epsilon^{2})$, which is bounded, and clips the result.
In the ray frame the point error is $\delta r\,\ray+r\,\delta\varphi\,\mathbf{u}$ with
$\mathbf{u}\perp\ray$ in the plane of incidence; its component along $\nrm$ is
$\delta r\cos\theta+r\,\delta\varphi\sin\theta$ up to sign, with variance
$\sigma_r^{2}\cos^{2}\theta+r^{2}\sigma_\varphi^{2}\sin^{2}\theta$, which is \eqref{eq:vn} and
remains bounded as $\theta\to\pi/2$.

\section{Pairwise Gate (Comparison Variant)}
\label{app:pairwise}
The pairwise gate replaces the shared surface by the consensus of the other sources of the
view and of its neighbours. For view $i$ the candidate sources are the $M$ estimator
outputs $(m,i)$; for each neighbour $j$ with provisional pose, the consensus depth of $j$
is warped into view $i$ and acts as an additional source that is never itself gated. For a
candidate $k$, let $\hat D_{-k}$ be the consensus of the other sources after a robust
scale-and-shift fit of each of them to a common reference, with variance $v_{-k}$ (the
inverse-variance combination of the aligned sources). The candidate is aligned to
$\hat D_{-k}$ by its own robust affine fit, and the statistic is \eqref{eq:Ts} with
$\hat S_i,v_{S,i}$ replaced by $\hat D_{-k},v_{-k}$. The consensus now has several members,
so a set is grown as in our earlier work: with $F_C=\prod_{i\in C}f_{i,u}$, $Z_C=\int F_C$,
$p_C=F_C/Z_C$ and $H(C)=-\!\int p_C\log p_C$, Algorithm~\ref{alg:pairwise} seeds with the pair
of largest pairwise evidence, expands with the compatible source of largest mean log Bayes
factor (which removes the order dependence), and returns the minimum-entropy set among
admissible sets with at most $F<M/2$ removals. Its cost is $C_{\mathrm{pair}}$ of
Section~\ref{sec:method:cost}.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Pairwise view-level admission (comparison variant)}
\label{alg:pairwise}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE aligned depth PDFs $\{f_{k,u}\}_{k=1}^{M}$ of one view with warped neighbours, removal budget $F<M/2$, threshold $\tau$
\STATE $(i^\star,j^\star)\gets\argmax_{i\ne j}\bar\ell_{j}(\{i\})$
\IF{$\bar\ell_{j^\star}(\{i^\star\})<\tau$}
  \RETURN no consensus
\ENDIF
\STATE $C\gets\{i^\star,j^\star\}$
\WHILE{$\exists\,k\notin C:\ \bar\ell_k(C)\ge\tau$}
  \STATE $k^\star\gets\argmax_{k\notin C}\bar\ell_k(C)$; \ $C\gets C\cup\{k^\star\}$
\ENDWHILE
\STATE Among admissible sets $C'$ with $M-|C'|\le F$, \textbf{return} $\argmin_{C'}H(C')$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
