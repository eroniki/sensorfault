\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
\textcolor{red}{\textbf{Only controlled experiments on one MatrixCity block have been run. The real-data
hypotheses H1--H6 are pending, and their planned tables and figures are in Supplement~\ref{S-sup:results}.}}

\subsection{E1: Controlled Fault Injection with Exact Depth}
\label{sec:results:e1}
E1 tests the statistical gate with exact truth. It uses MatrixCity block~3 \cite{data_matrixcity}: 185
frames on an $8\times8$ grid of positions in four headings, $30^{\circ}$ off nadir, at 300~m altitude,
rendered at $96\times54$ pixels. Surfels are placed on the exact geometry (81{,}152 surfels from 3~m voxels);
the measured depth is their rendered depth plus independent noise of 1\% of depth plus injected faults, so
the linearised fusion is exact. There is no splatting fit, pose refinement or real depth model. Faults are
rectangular offsets of $\delta\sigma$ on one view, or the same $8\sigma$ offset on a 25~m patch in three
neighbouring frames of a strip. Holm runs over $M=NR_{\max}=1850$ tests; the region scan uses $4\times6$
tiles. Fig.~\ref{fig:e1compact} shows the test on single views; the full summary (Table~\ref{S-tab:e1}) and
further figures are in Supplement~\ref{S-sup:e1full}.

The Holm-controlled loop kept the family-wise false alarm at zero in 20 clean scenes, and the scene
temperature kept it there when the noise was 30\% above its calibrated level; without the temperature,
every view was flagged. All single-view faults of 2--16$\sigma$ over 5--40\% of a view were detected, so
this grid does not separate the gate from the aligned-RMS baseline, which missed only 3 of 10 cases at
$2\sigma$ over 5\%. Two predictions of H6 do not hold in this setting. Leave-block-out ($B=3$) detected no
more of the three repeated faulty frames than $B=1$, because three frames supply only a small share of the
patch's coverage and did not mask each other. The outer loop did not raise detection either. What it
changed was false exclusions: a single pass that excludes every rejected view at once excluded 5--8\% of
clean views, neighbours \emph{swamped} by the faulty frames, whereas the loop excluded none. Two of the 30
repeated faulty frames were never detected. The energy statistic was slightly conservative (mean
$T/m=0.965$), and the single-test false-alarm rate at $p<0.05$ was 7.5\% with the temperature, which the
Holm schedule absorbs. Fused without the excluded view, the surface under the fault had a median height
error of $-0.16$~m, against $-2.16$~m with it and $-0.19$~m for the same noise without a fault
(Supplement~\ref{S-sec:results:surface}).

\subsection{H7: Pose Perturbation Check}
\label{sec:results:h7}
In the E1 setting, after the gate had excluded the faulty view, the input poses of chosen views (an
isolated view, a block of three, or the flagged view) were perturbed by $0.05$--$2^{\circ}$ and 1--50~GSD
(0.141~m at the median range of 327~m), two seeds and 16 perturbed views per level. Each view was tested
with the pose term $J_q\Sigma_qJ_q^{\top}$ of \eqref{eq:res}, and the poses were refined with the spline,
GNSS/IMU prior (0.3~m, $0.02^{\circ}$) and render-and-compare factors, all fixed before the run
(Supplement~\ref{S-sup:h7}).

\begin{figure*}[!t]
\centering
\input{\hfigdir/fig_h7.tex}
\caption{H7 on MatrixCity block~3 (E1 setting; 16 perturbed clean views per point, two seeds). Top: share of
perturbed clean views flagged by the gate before refinement. Bottom: median share of the pose error
removed by spline, pose-graph and render-and-compare refinement, per perturbed component, as specified
(solid) and in an exploratory variant without the perturbed frame's GNSS/IMU prior (dashed). Grey: the
range of each prediction; dotted: the 80\% target. In (c) and (f) rotation and translation are perturbed
together at paired levels (grey labels: translation in GSD).}
\label{fig:h7}
\end{figure*}

Prediction (a) holds (Fig.~\ref{fig:h7}a--c): the gate flagged every perturbed view at every level, and
none in the size-0 control. Its sensitivity even at $0.05^{\circ}$ and 1~GSD comes from building edges in
point-sampled exact depth, where 0.2--0.7\% of pixels see a different surfel and carry 97--99\% of the
squared standardised residual; a real depth model smooths edges, so this should not be expected there.
Perturbed views also swamped others: one perturbed group led Holm to reject 1.2 to 27.5 unperturbed views
on average in the same round, at most 67, which the outer loop (not run here) is designed to prevent.

Prediction (b) is falsified (Fig.~\ref{fig:h7}d--f): no level up to $1^{\circ}$ and 25~GSD reached a median
of 80\% removed. The refinement removed 63--64\% of a rotation and 38--80\% of a translation, leaving
$0.37^{\circ}$ and 9.9~GSD at $1^{\circ}$ and 25~GSD, and blocks recovered less than isolated views because
the shared perturbation also moves the spline. The main cause is the perturbed frame's own GNSS/IMU prior,
which holds the estimate near its faulty input: in an exploratory variant that drops this prior, as if the
gate's flag marked that reading as faulty, 85--89\% of rotations of $0.5^{\circ}$ and more were removed.
Per-level numbers are in Supplement~\ref{S-sup:h7full}.

\runin{Other checks.} A 2DGS model trained with gsplat on the clean RGB images of the same block reached
$23.1\pm4.3$~dB PSNR on 19 held-out views. As a surface it failed on water, 46\% of the area, which it placed
a median of 5.7~m too high, the kind of textureless region where image-based geometry fails
(Supplement~\ref{S-sec:results:rgb}).
