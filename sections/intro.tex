\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Monocular metric depth models \cite{est_metric3dv2,est_unidepth,est_depthpro} are now accurate
enough to supervise 3D reconstruction from images: depth-supervised Gaussian splatting
\cite{gs_3dgs,gs_2dgs,gs_dnsplatter} uses one predicted depth map per image as a geometric prior.
The predictions fail in a characteristic way. The failures are \emph{regional} and
\emph{appearance-driven}: on a reflective or glass surface the model predicts the depth of the
reflection, and on a textureless wall it has no cue and predicts a smooth guess. Inside such a
region the error is large, often many times the nominal noise, and outside it the same depth map
is good. Depth supervision bakes these regions into the surface, and nothing in a single image says
which depth map, or which part of it, to distrust.

The cheap defences act per pixel or per ray. Robust losses \cite{rob_blackrangarajan,rob_barron}
and distractor-aware splatting such as RobustNeRF and SpotLessSplats
\cite{gs_robustnerf,gs_spotless} down-weight residuals that look like outliers, mostly from
photometric evidence; a per-view RMS of the aligned depth residual, or the depth model's own
confidence, gives a view score. None of them states a false-alarm rate, and none uses the fact
that the other views of the same scene observe the same surface with known uncertainty.

\paragraph{Approach}
We propose a \emph{depth-only view gate} built on Gaussian surfels whose uncertainty renders. Each
2DGS-style surfel keeps its shape covariance for rendering and, separately, a $10\times10$
uncertainty over its mean, its tangent-space rotation error, its log scales and its opacity, with the
opacity frozen after a warm-up. Every view adds the alpha-weighted rasteriser information
$J^{\top}R^{-1}J$ to the surfels it sees, so the fused surface is a Laplace posterior obtained by
rendering, without correspondences. Leaving a view out is a subtraction of its information and one
Newton step. The gate renders the other views' surface, and its variance, into the tested view and
tests the whitened depth residuals: pixels that no other view sees are untestable, and the tested view
spawns no surfels of its own. The noise model has one free variable, a scene temperature, and the
depth model's systematic error is a distance term shared by all views. Colour is a separate
appearance block that may refine the poses but never updates the map geometry; it reaches geometry
only through the depth model.

\paragraph{What is and is not new}
The test is classical (Baarda's data snooping, Teunissen's DIA procedure \cite{stat_teunissen_dia} and
GNSS solution separation \cite{flt_raim_joerger}), and removing a view by subtraction is the cavity step of
expectation propagation \cite{mp_minka_ep}, used here once and without moment matching; surfels with confidence-weighted fusion are
point-based fusion \cite{fus_keller_surfels}; per-view alignment of monocular depth is standard
\cite{rec_monosdf}. What is new, to our knowledge, is a per-Gaussian uncertainty that the splatting
rasteriser renders into depth variance, used with exact leave-view-out to test whole views at a stated
false-alarm rate (Section~\ref{sec:related}).

\paragraph{Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Gaussian surfels with a separate $10\times10$ uncertainty (opacity frozen after a warm-up)
        whose normal-offset and tilt block renders into per-pixel depth variance, fused on rendering by
        $J^{\top}R^{-1}J$ with leave-view-out by subtraction and one Newton step (Sections~\ref{sec:method:surfels}--\ref{sec:method:fusion}).
  \item A depth-only view gate that tests whitened residuals against the other views' rendered
        surface, with untestable pixels and no spawning by the tested view
        (Section~\ref{sec:method:gate}).
  \item A pre-registered evaluation that starts by measuring the residuals of a real depth model,
        tests whether the model repeats its errors across views in textureless regions, and
        requires the gate to beat cheap baselines on regional, appearance-driven failures
        (Section~\ref{sec:setup}).
\end{itemize}
