\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\IEEEPARstart{M}{onocular} metric depth models \cite{est_metric3dv2,est_unidepth,est_depthpro} are accurate
enough to supervise Gaussian splatting with one depth map per image \cite{gs_3dgs,gs_2dgs,gs_dnsplatter},
which for aerial survey imagery could complement dense multi-view stereo. Their failures, however, are
\emph{regional} and \emph{appearance-driven}: on glass and water they return the depth of the reflection,
on textureless roofs and roads a smooth guess, many times the nominal noise, while the rest of the depth map
is accurate. Nadir views from survey altitude are also far from their mostly near-ground training data.
Depth supervision bakes such regions into the surface, and nothing in a single image says which depth map
to distrust. Robust losses \cite{rob_blackrangarajan,rob_barron} and distractor-aware splatting
\cite{gs_robustnerf,gs_spotless} down-weight outlying pixels, mostly on photometric evidence; none states a
false-alarm rate, and none uses the fact that the other views observe the same surface with known
uncertainty.

We propose a \emph{depth-only view gate} (Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline}). Each 2DGS surfel carries a
$10\times10$ information block, kept apart from its rendering shape, to which every view adds the
alpha-weighted rasteriser information $J^{\top}R^{-1}J$. Fusion is sparse and needs no correspondences,
unlike TSDF fusion \cite{fus_curless,fus_kinectfusion} or cost volumes \cite{rec_mvsnet}, and leaving out a
view, or a block of neighbouring frames that tend to share its errors, is a subtraction and one Newton
step. The gate renders the remaining views' surface and its variance into the tested view and tests its
whitened depth residuals at a stated false-alarm rate. The testing theory is classical
\cite{stat_teunissen_dia,flt_raim_joerger}; to our knowledge, what is new is a per-Gaussian uncertainty that
the rasteriser renders into depth variance, used with exact leave-out to test whole views. We contribute:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a depth-only view gate with untestable pixels, no surfels spawned by the tested view, and a
        Holm-controlled outer loop (Sections~\ref{sec:method:gate}--\ref{sec:method:loop});
  \item leave-block-out, designed so that neighbours that repeat a view's error cannot mask it; it is
        untested so far on real repeated failures (H2) (Section~\ref{sec:method:fusion});
  \item controlled experiments on false-alarm control, swamping and pose errors, and a pre-registered
        protocol for real depth models (Sections~\ref{sec:setup}--\ref{sec:results}).
\end{itemize}
