\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\runin{Testing theory.} Baarda's data snooping, its reliability theory and the DIA procedure test a
linear Gaussian model with nuisance parameters for misspecified observations and give a minimum
detectable bias \cite{stat_teunissen_dia}; receiver autonomous integrity monitoring applies them to
satellites, and solution separation equals the residual-based test \cite{flt_raim_joerger}. Our gate
is an instance: the observations are pixels, the nuisance is a per-view scale and offset, and the
leave-one-out solution is a rendered surface.

\runin{Surfels, splatting and monocular priors.} Point-based fusion keeps surfels with a normal, a
radius and a confidence and fuses depth by projective association \cite{fus_keller_surfels}. Gaussian
splatting renders primitives with a differentiable rasteriser \cite{gs_3dgs,gs_gsplat}; 2DGS uses planar
Gaussians for accurate surfaces \cite{gs_2dgs}, and monocular depth and normal priors supervise
splatting and implicit surfaces after per-view alignment \cite{gs_dnsplatter,rec_monosdf}. We fuse by
the rasteriser's own Jacobians rather than by association, and keep the uncertainty apart from the
rendering shape.

\runin{Robust reconstruction.} Robust losses \cite{rob_blackrangarajan,rob_barron} and distractor-aware
methods \cite{gs_robustnerf,gs_spotless,gs_robustsplat} down-weight pixels whose residual, mostly
photometric, looks like an outlier; multi-view consistency filters test pixels by correspondence
\cite{fus_merrell,fus_colmap_mvs}. None decides on a whole view at a stated false-alarm rate.

\runin{Uncertainty in radiance fields.} FisherRF uses the Fisher information of radiance-field
parameters for view selection and uncertainty quantification \cite{unc_fisherrf}; Bayes' Rays fits a
post-hoc Laplace uncertainty over a perturbation field of a trained NeRF \cite{unc_bayesrays}. They
quantify uncertainty; we render a per-primitive uncertainty into depth variance and use it, with exact
leave-view-out, to test views. H6 puts both inside the same test. A longer review is in
Supplement~\ref{sup:related}.
