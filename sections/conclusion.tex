\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We have proposed a depth-only view gate in which each surfel's uncertainty is rendered into depth variance,
leave-out is an exact subtraction, and views are tested against the others' surface at a stated false-alarm
rate under a Holm schedule. With exact depth, the loop held the false alarm at zero and avoided swamping;
pose errors were detected but not fully recovered. The rendered variance is overconfident because
cross-surfel correlations are ignored, errors repeated across whole strips are invisible, and pixels seen
by no other view are untestable (Supplement~\ref{S-sup:limits}).
