\section{Conclusions, Limitations and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We have proposed a depth-only view gate for depth-supervised Gaussian splatting. Each surfel keeps a
$9\times9$ uncertainty, separate from its rendering shape, whose normal-offset and tilt block the
rasteriser renders into depth variance; views add their alpha-weighted information, leaving a view
out is a subtraction, and the tested view is compared with the other views' rendered surface by a
whitened test with a stated false-alarm rate. The test is Baarda's and Teunissen's; the
contribution is an uncertainty that renders and its use for testing views. The evaluation starts by
measuring the depth model and requires the gate to beat cheap baselines on regional,
appearance-driven failures. None of H1--H6 has been tested.

\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:disc:limits}
(1)~Independent per-surfel blocks ignore cross-surfel correlations, the correlation of depth error
within a view and the shared pose error of a view, so the rendered variance is overconfident; the
threshold is calibrated on clean data, and H5 measures the gap. (2)~If the depth model repeats the
same error across views, as H2 tests for textureless walls, the other views agree with the fault and
the gate cannot see it. (3)~Pixels that no other view sees are untestable, so low overlap lowers
availability. (4)~Several faulty views supporting the same region can make the leave-view-out surface
follow the fault. (5)~The fusion is linearised at the fitted surfels; leave-view-out is exact only for
the linearised model, and the alpha weights and the set of surfels come from a fit that used every
view. (6)~Whole-view affine errors are absorbed by the alignment and are neither detected nor
harmful. (7)~Colour never updates geometry, so appearance cues are not used for detection, and the
gate depends on the depth model being right in most views. (8)~Poses come from the datasets; pose
faults are out of scope. (9)~The measurement model is calibrated on a few static scenes for one
default model.

\subsection{Future Work}
\label{sec:disc:future}
Planned, none started: modelling cross-surfel correlation by low-rank or banded blocks; testing poses
as well as depth; using the rendered uncertainty for view selection and for reconstruction from fewer
views; online operation; and depth from physical sensors.
