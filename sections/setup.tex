\section{Experimental Design and Hypotheses}
\label{sec:setup}
The experiments ask when the gate helps and when it does not, starting with a measurement of the depth
model. They use public datasets, released pretrained models and public code, and the protocol, hypotheses
and thresholds are tagged in the code repository before any test scene is run. So far E1 and the H7 pose
check have been run, both on one MatrixCity block (Section~\ref{sec:results}).

\runin{Data and models.} The main real set is UseGeo \cite{data_usegeo}, UAV nadir survey strips with
exterior orientation and per-image LiDAR depth; H3D \cite{data_h3d} is a second real set if its raw images
and orientations can be obtained. MatrixCity \cite{data_matrixcity}, a synthetic city with exact depth and
poses for aerial views, is used for fault injection with exact truth, and ETH3D \cite{data_eth3d}, with
laser depth and registered poses, is a sanity check. Depth Pro \cite{est_depthpro} is the default depth
model; Metric3D v2 and UniDepthV2 supply the confidence baseline, and all three enter a comparison of nadir
views from altitude with near-ground views.

\runin{Faults, baselines and metrics.} Natural failures are labelled against the reference depth on
connected regions and classed as specular, textureless or other. Injected faults (offset, plane
replacement, blurred copy, mirrored depth) cover 2--40\% of a view at 2--16$\sigma$, independent per view
or repeated across neighbours. Baselines are SpotLessSplats \cite{gs_spotless}, the RobustNeRF loss
\cite{gs_robustnerf} and robust depth losses in the fit; the aligned RMS residual and the depth model's
confidence as view tests; and no gate and an oracle gate. Metrics are detection power at 5\% per-view false
alarm, false-alarm and untestable rates, held-out AbsRel, and surface accuracy, completeness and F-score.
Details are in Supplement~\ref{S-sup:setup}.

\runin{Hypotheses.} Each states what would falsify it (full text and thresholds in
Supplement~\ref{S-sup:hyp}).
\begin{description}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item[H1] \emph{Go/no-go:} on UseGeo and ETH3D development scenes, $\ge10\%$ of views carry a failure
        covering $\ge2\%$ of pixels at $>3\sigma$, and keeping them costs $\ge2$ points of F-score.
  \item[H2] \emph{Repetition:} cross-view error correlation is $\ge0.5$ on textureless regions and
        $\le0.3$ on specular ones, and decays along the strip; the decay sets $B$.
  \item[H3] \emph{Detection:} at 5\% false alarm the gate beats the best baseline by $\ge15$ points of power
        on specular failures; no gain is predicted on textureless ones.
  \item[H4] \emph{Reconstruction:} AbsRel and F-score improve by $\ge10\%$ on failure scenes and change by
        $\le2\%$ on clean scenes.
  \item[H5] \emph{Calibration:} the per-view false-alarm rate stays within $[0.5\alpha,1.5\alpha]$ on test
        scenes without retuning.
  \item[H6] \emph{Ablations:} each design choice (cavity, whitening, scene temperature, outer loop,
        leave-block-out) earns a stated margin.
  \item[H7] \emph{Poses:} (a)~the gate flags $\ge80\%$ of views perturbed by $\ge0.5^{\circ}$ or 10 ground
        sampling distances (GSD); (b)~refinement removes $\ge80\%$ of perturbations up to $1^{\circ}$ and
        25~GSD.
\end{description}
