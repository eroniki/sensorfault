\section{Experimental Design and Hypotheses}
\label{sec:setup}

The experiments evaluate the gate as an integrity monitor and ask when view-level gating helps
and when it does not. All use public datasets, public pretrained models and public code, and the
protocol, hypotheses and thresholds are fixed and tagged in the code repository before any test
scene is run. No experiment has been run for this version.

\subsection{Design in Brief}
\label{sec:setup:expsetup}
\emph{Data.} ETH3D \cite{data_eth3d} (laser-scan reference depth, which labels natural failures
without injection) is primary; ScanNet++ \cite{data_scannetpp} (subject to its access terms) is
secondary; Replica \cite{data_replica} gives controlled synthetic scenes. \emph{Depth model.}
One metric model per image, Depth Pro \cite{est_depthpro} by default (absolute scale, no
intrinsics needed); UniDepth and Metric3D v2 appear only in the ensemble ablation and as
confidence-covariate variants. \emph{Faults.} Injected coherent bias on a fraction $f\le0.8$ of a
view plus the affine control $f=1$, smooth warps, region failures and non-affine distortion,
spoofed pose priors, glitches and false loop closures, together with natural failures.
\emph{Baselines.} Robust kernels (including switchable constraints, dynamic covariance scaling
and max-mixtures), soft per-view down-weighting, the COLMAP geometric-consistency filter with a
pixel-count test, plain voting, no gate, an oracle gate, and a pairwise gate; MASt3R-SfM, VGGT and
COLMAP SfM are context only. \emph{Metrics.} Detection power at 5\% false alarm, empirical
minimum detectable bias, false-alarm rate, no-consensus rate and availability, trajectory and
fused-surface error, and protection-level coverage. The measurement model is calibrated once per
depth model on clean development data. Details are in Supplement~\ref{sup:setup}.

\subsection{Hypotheses}
\label{sec:setup:hyp}
Each hypothesis states what would falsify it, and several state where the gate is expected
\emph{not} to help; thresholds and falsifiers are given in full in Supplement~\ref{sup:hyp}.
\begin{description}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
  \item[H1 (regime map).] At 5\% false alarm the empirical minimum detectable bias follows
        \eqref{eq:mdb} from the measured $\Sigma_r$ within a factor of 2; in the sub-noise regime
        ($\delta\le0.5$, $0.2\le f\le0.8$) the gate detects $\ge80\%$ of faulty views and the best
        pixel filter, robust kernel and soft weight $\le20\%$; in the sparse-outlier regime
        ($\delta\ge4$, $f\le0.02$) the pixel filter wins or ties; a uniform bias ($f=1$) is neither
        detected nor harmful.
  \item[H2 (consequence).] In the sub-noise regime gating cuts trajectory and fused-surface error by
        $\ge25\%$ against the best baseline and lands within 20\% of the oracle gate; in the
        sparse regime baselines tie or win.
  \item[H3 (statistic).] The evidence criterion beats the chi-square gate by $\ge10$ points only where
        its assumptions break (otherwise a tie, Proposition~\ref{prop:loo}); the region scan beats the
        energy statistic for a confined bias and loses for a diffuse warp (20 points each way).
  \item[H4 (leave-view-out, alignment, support).] Subtracting the view, aligning, and masking by
        support keep clean false alarm $\le5\%$; a joint surface loses $\ge10$ points of power.
  \item[H5 (placement).] Gating before pose graph optimisation beats gating after it and soft
        per-view down-weighting in the sub-noise regime; gate plus kernel is at least as good as either.
  \item[H6 (adequacy and price).] On fault-free views mean $T_i/(n-2)=1\pm0.1$ and false alarm lies
        in $[0.5\alpha,1.5\alpha]$ without per-scene retuning; the price of gating on clean data is
        at most 5\% error with availability $\ge90\%$; reference points are context only.
  \item[H7 (shared surface).] The closed-form surface gate is linear in $N$ and $\ge2\times$
        faster than the pairwise gate at $N\ge100$, $\bar d\ge10$, within 5 points of its power, and
        within 3 points of the fixed-budget MCMC surface; per-region outputs are reported with
        coverage; view order is immaterial.
  \item[H8 (pose faults).] The gate detects $\ge80\%$ of views above the pose minimum detectable
        bias and beats kernels for small coherent offsets; loop-closure baselines match it on large
        false loops.
  \item[H9 (protection level, optional).] Single-fault exceedance of the protection level is
        $\le\beta_{\mathrm{md}}+5$ points under its assumptions and $>2\beta_{\mathrm{md}}$ when they fail.
  \item[H10 (ensemble ablation).] Models sharing training data fail on the same views (lift $\ge2$);
        cross-model voting misses common-mode failures that the gate catches; the ensemble gate adds
        $\ge5$ points.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Breakdown scenarios.} Reported as honest negatives: model-level faults; very few
views; faults below the detection floor and small $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$; sparse outliers; low
overlap; faulty views that dominate a region; association failure and cold start; large false
loop closures; misspecified measurement model; and poor provisional poses (Supplement~\ref{sup:setup}).
