\subsection{Setting}
Posed views $\{(I_i, K_i, T_i)\}_{i=1}^N$ of a static scene. For a tested view $j$ the model produces samples of its depth.

\subsection{Affine-invariant depth diffusion}
\label{sec:model}
A pretrained latent image diffusion model is fine-tuned to sample the depth of a view conditioned on its RGB latent. The target is the
per-view percentile-normalised depth; global scale and shift are not predicted and no camera embedding is used.

\subsection{Unguided and guided sampling}
\label{sec:guidance}
\emph{Unguided} samples use only $I_j$ and give the measurement with its own covariance. \emph{Guided} samples add, at the last denoising
steps, a likelihood gradient toward the depth rendered from a Gaussian-splat surface fitted to the other views, after a closed-form per-step
scale and shift alignment. The render for view $j$ uses nothing derived from view $j$.

\subsection{Leave-view-out statistic with a fitted scale and shift}
\label{sec:statistic}
Let $\bar m$ be the mean of $M$ unguided samples aligned to each other, $\Sigma_U$ their local-shape covariance, and $D^{-j}$, $\Sigma_r^{-j}$ the
render from the other views and its variance. Fit only a per-view scale and shift,
\begin{equation}
(s,t)=\argmin_{s,t}\ \lVert s\,\bar m + t\mathbf 1 - D^{-j}\rVert^{2}_{(s^{2}(1+1/M)\Sigma_U+\Sigma_r^{-j})^{-1}},
\end{equation}
and test the residual local shape
$T=r^\top \bigl(s^{2}(1+1/M)\Sigma_U+\Sigma_r^{-j}\bigr)^{-1} r$, $r=s\bar m+t\mathbf 1-D^{-j}$.

\subsection{Semi-self-supervised training}
\label{sec:training}
Labelled views use the standard diffusion loss on normalised ground-truth depth. Unlabelled views use a scale-and-shift-invariant
render-and-compare loss against the leave-view-out render, weighted by the render variance, plus photometric consistency to the other views.
