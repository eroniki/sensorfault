# A. Diffusion-based monocular depth (all 10 verified via arXiv abstract page, fetched 2026-11-16)

Venue = what the arXiv "Comments" field states; arXiv-only entries had no venue listed on the page.

1. **ke2024marigold** (CVPR 2024, arXiv:2312.02145). Fine-tunes Stable Diffusion for affine-invariant relative depth from synthetic data; ensembles multiple samples at test time. Ours: same affine-invariant target and diffusion backbone, but we keep samples, calibrate their spread, and condition on multi-view 2DGS renders; Marigold uses the ensemble only to sharpen a point estimate. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02145. Caveat: arXiv comment says "CVPR 2024 camera ready".
2. **garciamartin2025e2edepth** (WACV 2025 Oral, arXiv:2409.11355). Shows a fixed single-step, deterministic fine-tuning of the diffusion backbone matches/beats multi-step Marigold-style inference. Ours: deliberately stochastic; single-step end-to-end removes the sampling distribution we need. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11355. Caveat: exact title differs from the "Marigold-LCM" wording; this is the e2e fine-tune paper. I did not open the Marigold-LCM release, so it is not cited.
3. **he2024lotus** (arXiv-only on page, arXiv:2409.18124). Diffusion foundation model for depth/normal with x0-prediction and a single-step deterministic mode. Ours: point estimate, no calibrated spread or multi-view guidance. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18124. Caveat: may have a later venue version I did not verify.
4. **fu2024geowizard** (arXiv-only on page, arXiv:2403.12013). Joint depth+normal diffusion with geometric switcher over scene domains. Ours: per-view depth only, but with multi-view consistency from other views. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12013. Caveat: ECCV 2024 publication not verified here.
5. **gui2025depthfm** (AAAI 2025, arXiv:2403.13788). Flow matching from image to depth, few-step inference, also offers an uncertainty estimate from sample variation (stated in its abstract? not re-checked; do not claim without reading). URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13788. Caveat: the uncertainty claim is unverified.
6. **saxena2023ddvm** (NeurIPS 2023 Oral, arXiv:2306.01923). Generic pixel-space diffusion for optical flow and depth with self-supervised pretraining plus synthetic supervision; handles noisy/incomplete training depth via infilling and step-unrolling. Ours: closest in spirit on sampling and semi-self-supervised training; they target metric depth, a point estimate/multi-sample mean, and no multi-view guidance. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01923.
7. **saxena2023depthgen** (arXiv-only on page, arXiv:2302.14816). Early diffusion depth (DepthGen); multimodal depth samples, depth infilling. Ours: they show multi-hypothesis sampling but do not evaluate calibration or use multi-view renders. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.14816. Caveat: abstract not re-read for the claims above; only metadata verified.
8. **hu2024depthcrafter** (arXiv-only on page, arXiv:2409.02095). Video-diffusion depth for long temporally consistent sequences. Ours: temporal consistency along a video, not multi-view consistency with a 3D surface; no uncertainty. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02095. Caveat: CVPR 2025 version not verified.
9. **ke2024rollingdepth** (arXiv-only on page, arXiv:2411.19189). Turns a single-image latent diffusion depth model into a video depth estimator via snippets and global co-alignment. Ours: aligns per-snippet affine ambiguity (scale/shift) similar in spirit to our per-view fit; but temporal, point estimate. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.19189. Caveat: ICCV 2025 version not verified.
10. **xu2025pixelperfect** (NeurIPS 2025, arXiv:2510.07316). Pixel-space diffusion transformer with semantic prompts to avoid VAE flying pixels. Ours: latent SDXL+LoRA; their sharp boundaries are complementary, not an uncertainty method. URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07316.

Caveats on content lines: venues/titles/authors/years were verified from the abstract pages; the one-line method descriptions come from my background knowledge and were not all checked against abstracts (items 5, 7 explicitly flagged).

## Uncertainty / ensembling in this line of work
Marigold-style methods treat sampling noise as a nuisance: several stochastic samples are aligned and median-merged into one depth map, and the spread is at most shown as a qualitative confidence map without calibration. Single-step variants (e2e fine-tuning, Lotus deterministic mode) drop stochasticity entirely for speed. DDVM and DepthGen are the main works that embrace the multimodal sample distribution (e.g. infilling, multiple hypotheses), but they do not report calibration of the spread. Video methods (DepthCrafter, RollingDepth) spend their sampling budget on temporal coherence and global alignment of affine ambiguity, not uncertainty. To our knowledge from this slice, none checks samples against an independent leave-view-out 3D surface or evaluates spread as calibrated uncertainty; this should be stated cautiously as a gap within the papers reviewed.
