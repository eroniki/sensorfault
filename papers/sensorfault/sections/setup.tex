\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:setup}
Hypotheses, metrics and thresholds are fixed on development data and tagged in the code repository before
any held-out tile or test scene is scored. Only public data and released models are used, and no fault
label enters any fit.

\runin{Data.} MatrixCity \cite{data_matrixcity} gives exact depth and poses for aerial strips; one block is
the development set, split into calibration and held-out ground tiles of 64~m with a buffer.
\TODO{State the test blocks once obtained; with one block the tables are marked ``same scene''.}
ETH3D \cite{data_eth3d} supplies real images with laser depth (13 scenes). Depth Pro \cite{est_depthpro} is
the primary depth model; Metric3D~v2, UniDepthV2 and Depth Anything~V2
\cite{est_metric3dv2,est_unidepth,est_dav2} set the scope.

\runin{Implementation.} Surfels are rasterized with gsplat \cite{gs_gsplat}; the records
\eqref{eq:depthrecord} and \eqref{eq:imagerecord} are accumulated from its backward pass. Fitting and judging frames alternate along each strip, so both sets span the
same baselines. \TODO{Hardware and time per frame.}

\runin{Stages.} The experiments switch on the terms of \eqref{eq:loss} one at a time
(Fig.~\ref{fig:overview}). Stage~0 checks the factorization \eqref{eq:factor} against central finite
differences on an independent reference renderer. Stage~A fits the surfels with the depth term alone and measures the overconfidence of
the depth record (H1, H2). Stage~B adds the photometric term with projected appearance and calibrates the
image record by injection (H3, H4). Stage~C uses both: the score test and the refit map (H5--H7).

\runin{Faults.} Natural failures are the depth model's own. Injected faults replace the depth inside a
region of \ph{16, 64 or 256} surfels by that of the plane displaced by $\boldsymbol\delta$: by the same
$\boldsymbol\delta$ in every frame (repeated), by independent draws of equal size (independent), or in half
of the frames (partial), at five magnitudes from \ph{0.5} to \ph{8} single-frame standard deviations.
Map-level injection, which moves surfels and re-renders, calibrates the pixel information
(Section~\ref{sec:method:honest}) and measures its capture range.

\runin{Baselines.} Depth only; a depth-consensus gate that tests each depth map against the surface of
the others with a block of neighboring frames left out \cite{stat_teunissen_dia,flt_raim_joerger}; a
fixed and a decaying depth weight; information fusion without saturation; selection of the prior by a
rendered-depth consistency mask \cite{dep_indepthwetrust} and by a photometric mask
\cite{dep_reliability}, both reimplemented from their descriptions; a single Fisher information field in
the style of \cite{unc_fisherrf}.

\runin{Metrics.} The standardized error $z_k$ of Section~\ref{sec:method:fusion} has $z_k^2\sim\chi^2_3$
if the map is honest. We report its robust scale $s_z$ (1 if honest), coverage at the nominal 95\%, and
the surface log-score. A segment is faulty if it lies in an injected region and its true error exceeds
twice the single-frame standard deviation; detection is the true-positive rate at 5\% false alarms on
healthy segments of disjoint tiles. Intervals are 95\% percentile bootstrap intervals with the 64~m tile
as the resampling unit.

\runin{Hypotheses.} A hypothesis is supported or refuted only if the interval of its metric lies wholly on
one side of its threshold.
\begin{description}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
  \item[H1] \emph{Overconfidence:} $s_z$ of a depth-only map exceeds 1.25.
  \item[H2] \emph{The law:} the realized fused variance is within $[0.67,1.5]$ of
        $\varrho+(1-\varrho)/n$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:law}).
  \item[H3] \emph{Measurement:} the image record recovers injected displacements up to the size the prior
        leaves, with relative error at most 0.25; the factorization \eqref{eq:factor} matches finite
        differences.
  \item[H4] \emph{Power:} realistic faults carry a noncentrality of at least 11.
  \item[H5] \emph{Detection:} on repeated faults the tension reaches an area under the curve of at
        least 0.9 where the consensus gate stays at or below 0.6.
  \item[H6] \emph{Null:} on healthy segments the mean of $T_q/r$ lies in $[0.85,1.15]$.
  \item[H7] \emph{Calibration:} the fused map has $s_z$ in $[0.8,1.25]$ and coverage within 0.04 of the
        nominal 95\%.
\end{description}
